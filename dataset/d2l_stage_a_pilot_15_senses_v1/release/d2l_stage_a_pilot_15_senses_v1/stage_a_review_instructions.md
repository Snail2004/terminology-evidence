# Stage A Pilot Review Instructions

Review only the assigned sense and the real D2L English contexts in this pack.
Do not use another reviewer’s fields, Global scores, or downstream decisions.

R0 cases use source-ground review plus the separate blind-audit template.
R1/R2 cases require at least one human reviewer. R3 cases require two
distinct blind human reviewers. R4 and E-unjudgeable cases require
adjudication when the review policy says so.

Leave fields blank when you cannot judge. Do not mark a record COMPLETE
without real reviewer provenance. Positive evidence must be SAME_SENSE
PRIMARY/BACKUP D2L English context; contrastive context is boundary-only.
